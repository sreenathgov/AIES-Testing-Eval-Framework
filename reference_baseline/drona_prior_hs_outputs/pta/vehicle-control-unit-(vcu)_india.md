## Pre-Classification Note: 8471 vs 8537 Distinction

Chapter 84 Note 5(E) excludes dedicated controllers from heading 8471 in India as well. The VCU is a dedicated vehicle control system → NOT 8471 → heading 8537 applies.

## GRI Analysis

**Resolved at GRI 1.** India ITC-HS heading 8537.10 covers control and distribution boards at ≤1,000V. India's tariff schedule does not subdivide 8537.10 further at the 8-digit level — single entry 85371000 applies to all control boards and panels within this voltage range.
