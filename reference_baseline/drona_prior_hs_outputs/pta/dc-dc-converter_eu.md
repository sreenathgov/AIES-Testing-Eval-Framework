## GRI Analysis

**Resolved at GRI 1.** EU CN 8504.40 covers static converters. EU Regulation 2025/1926 does not create a dedicated CN subheading for DC-DC converters — standard 8504.40 subheadings apply. EU CN 8-digit (.84 or .90) depends on power rating specification.

## Classification

- **Code**: 8504.40.84 or 8504.40.90 (8-digit requires power rating)
- **Subheading text**: Static converters
- **Heading text**: Electrical transformers, static converters (for example, rectifiers) and inductors
