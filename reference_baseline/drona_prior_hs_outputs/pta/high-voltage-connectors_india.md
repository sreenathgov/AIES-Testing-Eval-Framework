## GRI Analysis

**Resolved at GRI 1.** India ITC-HS heading 8536.69 covers plugs and sockets, further divided by material:
- 85366910: Of plastic
- 85366990: Of other materials

The High-Voltage Connectors have:
- Housing: PBT GF30 (glass-fiber reinforced PBT) — a high-performance thermoplastic (plastic)
- Contacts: Silver-plated copper — metal

India's subdivision is by housing/body material. However, the contacts (silver-plated copper) are the electrically functional element. Where the connector body is plastic but contacts are metal, India customs practice has applied 85366990 ("of other materials") when the metal contacts are integral to the connector's identity and function.

Classification at 8536.69.90 reflects the mixed-material nature with metal contacts as the functional element. An alternative reading (housing = plastic → 85366910) is possible and would not affect heading-level classification.

## Classification

- **Code**: 8536.69.90
- **Subheading text**: Plugs and sockets — of other materials
- **Heading text**: Electrical apparatus for switching or protecting electrical circuits or for making connections to or in electrical circuits... for a voltage not exceeding 1,000 V
- **Alternative**: 8536.69.10 (of plastic housing) — heading-level classification unchanged
