## GRI Analysis

**Resolved at GRI 1.** India ITC-HS heading 8537.10.00 covers boards and panels equipped with two or more apparatus of heading 8535 or 8536, for electric control or the distribution of electricity, for a voltage not exceeding 1,000V.

India's 8537.10 is a single subheading (85371000) without further subdivision at the 8-digit level. The PDU fits the heading text directly — it is a distribution unit housing fuses and relays for HV current distribution in the EV powertrain.
