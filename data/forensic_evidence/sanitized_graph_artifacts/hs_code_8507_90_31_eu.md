## Classification Node: 8507.90.31 (EU CN 2026)

EU CN 2026 (Regulation 2025/1926), codifying EU Commission Note C/2025/6096: PE/PP microporous separator films with thickness <40μm are classified as "parts of electric accumulators — separators" under 8507.90.31.

### Resolution Basis

Commission Note C/2025/6096: at <40μm, PE/PP separator film has no commercial application outside lithium-ion battery cells → it is exclusively a battery part. The function-first approach overrides the general plastics route (3921.19) for EU imports.

Thickness threshold: **<40μm**. Commercial battery-grade separators (10-25μm) universally satisfy this threshold.

### Jurisdiction Divergence

**US classifies the same product under 3921.19.0000** (cellular plastics, CBP HQ 967313). This is a genuine, legally entrenched Chapter-level divergence.
**India classifies under 3921.19.90** (cellular plastics, same approach as US).
