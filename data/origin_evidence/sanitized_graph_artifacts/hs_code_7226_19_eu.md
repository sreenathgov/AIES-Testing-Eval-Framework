## Classification Node: 7226.19.10 (EU CN)

EU CN 7226.19 further splits at 0.5mm thickness:
- 7226.19.10: thickness <0.5mm
- 7226.19.80: thickness ≥0.5mm

Motor-grade NOES (0.27mm or 0.35mm standard grades for EV motors) → thickness <0.5mm → **7226.19.10**.

Width condition still applies: slit coil <600mm → 7226; master coil ≥600mm → 7225.
