## Classification Node: 8708.99.97 (EU CN — 8-digit)

EU CN 8708.99.97 is the terminal "Other" subheading under 8708.99 for parts and accessories of motor vehicles not classified as closed-die forged steel parts (8708.99.93) and not covered by industrial assembly end-use provisions (8708.99.10).

### Resolution Basis

The subheading cascade under EU CN 8708.99 operates as follows:
- **8708.99.10** — Parts for the industrial assembly of designated vehicle classes (requires authorized end-use customs procedure)
- **8708.99.93** — Of closed-die forged steel (material-specific; excludes multi-material assemblies)
- **8708.99.97** — Other (terminal catch-all)

Integrated and hyper-integrated e-axles are multi-material assemblies (cast aluminum housing, copper windings, rare-earth permanent magnets, silicon carbide semiconductors) — they fail the closed-die forged steel condition of 8708.99.93. Absent an authorized end-use procedure, the default statutory classification cascades to **8708.99.97**.

EU OJ records (Official Journal, Autonomous Tariff Suspensions) confirm this pattern: complex gearboxes and torque distribution transfer cases in aluminum housings are classified under 8708.99.97.
